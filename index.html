Case 01 · Research infrastructure

dataWASHES

GESID / open research infrastructure

Platform ↗ Dashboard ↗ Source ↗

dataWASHES is a research infrastructure initiative maintained by GESID to organize and make accessible bibliographic information about the Workshop on Social, Human and Economic Aspects of Software (WASHES). It brings together records from editions spanning 2016–2025 through a public platform and dashboard.

The work involves a collaborative pipeline for collecting, processing, validating, and presenting bibliographic data, supporting access to a research community's publication history.

Institutional ownership: GESID. The platform and its data are collaborative outputs; the contribution below refers to my own work.

Case 02 · Empirical study

Constructive Patterns for Human-Centered Tech Hiring

CHASE 2026 · Co-located with ICSE

Proceedings ↗ Preprint ↗ Study page ↗

This study investigates how early-career software engineers experience online recruitment and selection. Based on interviews and reported recruitment experiences, the research identifies constructive patterns that can inform more human-centered hiring practices.
