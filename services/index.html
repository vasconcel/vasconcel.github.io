[02] Scope & Boundaries

Scope, boundaries and ethics

Clear commercial terms and scientific integrity before any work begins.

Engagement model

Closed scope defined in writing before work starts, budget quoted on demand (no open-ended hourly billing), delivery by milestones, and code and documentation handed to the client.

No academic ghostwriting

I do not write papers, undergraduate theses, master's theses, or doctoral dissertations, and I do not perform interpretative qualitative or thematic analysis in place of the researcher.

Responsible collection

No bypassing aggressive anti-bot protections or CAPTCHAs, and no collection of private data without authorization.

Infrastructure and support

No custody of infrastructure in personal accounts and no 24/7 support SLA after handover. Hosting stays in the client's account; later maintenance is agreed separately.
